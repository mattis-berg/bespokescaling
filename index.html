    Case Studies

    A selection of projects where we helped companies unlock new pipeline, revenue and growth with performance-driven marketing.

    B2B SaaS – HR Tech

    Industry: HR Tech

    Challenge

    • Demo volume flat for months.
    • High CPL from Meta & LinkedIn.
    • Low sales acceptance on paid leads.

    Solution

    • Repositioned offer around HR decision-maker pain.
    • Full-funnel Meta & LinkedIn structure (cold, warm, retargeting).
    • New demo landing page and CRM-based lead routing.

    Results

    • +185% more qualified demos.
    • 42% lower CPL across core markets.
    • 4.1x ROAS within 60 days.

    Lifestyle Brand – DTC

    Industry: DTC E-commerce (Lifestyle)

    Challenge

    • ROAS collapsed when scaling above €1,500/day.
    • Creative fatigue and weak hooks.
    • Checkout leaking high-intent traffic.

    Solution

    • Modular UGC creative system for Meta.
    • Rebuilt campaign structure by funnel stage.
    • CRO fixes on product and checkout pages.

    Results

    • 300% growth in monthly revenue.
    • +72% ROAS while scaling.
    • €7,000/day in profitable ad spend.

    Cybersecurity SaaS – Meta Leads

    Industry: B2B SaaS (Security)

    Challenge

    • Inbound pipeline too small for sales targets.
    • Previous Meta tests produced few, weak leads.
    • Brand unknown to IT and security leadership.

    Solution

    • Meta-only play targeting IT leaders, CISOs and security buyers.
    • UGC explainers, problem–solution ads and authority-driven messaging.
    • Dedicated landing page integrated into their CRM.

    Results

    • +263% increase in inbound pipeline.
    • CPL cut from €148 → €54.
    • 41 qualified demo calls in 45 days.
